# Analyze Atlas SSO and Audit

1. Identify organization/project identity model, IdP/federation configuration, groups, and role mappings.
2. Inspect Atlas administrative access separately from database authentication.
3. Evaluate least privilege and lifecycle of team/group assignments.
4. Inspect audit capabilities and relevant events available for the deployed Atlas tier/configuration.
5. Correlate access changes with incident timelines.
6. Never expose SAML assertions, secrets, or tokens.

## Output
- Identity Federation
- Role Mapping
- Audit Evidence
- Governance Gaps
- Recommended Action
