# Analyze Backup and PITR

1. Identify deployment, backup configuration, snapshot schedule, retention, and RPO/RTO.
2. Inspect continuous backup/PITR capability for the deployed tier and configuration.
3. Evaluate cross-region or cross-project recovery requirements where supported.
4. Verify restore readiness through tested recovery procedures rather than snapshot existence alone.
5. Identify gaps between business RPO/RTO and configured protection.
6. Do not restore over production without explicit authorization.

## Output
- Backup Configuration
- PITR/Retention
- Recovery Readiness
- RPO/RTO Gaps
- Recommended Action
